Sonification of Primes

a generative instrument built on quadratic residues modulo primes — a first attempt to let mathematics sound

Focus — 1:30 — parts parts: one piece, two voices — Markov sings his voices and the shared bed, Markova hers. Focus: a proof — thesis, lemmas, conclusion; the tension climbs, never a beat. Meditation — palindrome — 2:30 palindrome: the score plays forward on one half and back, note for note, on the other. Meditation: a prayer in one large prime — the approach to the centre, the centre held, the thirds. Chores — retro — 2:00 retro: the retrograde canon — the answer plays the same material reversed, live, from the mirror residue. Chores: humour and lightness — a toy kit of clicks, zaps and claps that re-arranges every scene.
